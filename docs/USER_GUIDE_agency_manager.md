# User guide: agency manager (several buildings)

You run buildings for owners and committees. One sign-in covers every building of your agency, including buildings added later.

## Portfolio

- **Portfolio** lists every building: flats, occupancy, city. **Add a building** (name, address, city, floors) and **Open building** to work inside one.
- The **building box** at the top switches between buildings at any time. What you do in one building never shows in another. Each building has its own plan, charges, committee, staff and data.
- You hold the powers of a property admin in every building: flats and people, charges and invoices, payments, expenses, staff and payroll, notices, onboarding.
- You can also download the whole building as a ZIP from **Data export**.

## Setting up each building

Use the same steps as a property admin (see `USER_GUIDE_building_admin.md`): flats (generate or import), charges, due day, committee. The **setup wizard** appears for a new, empty building and skips what is already known.

## Agency staff and duty roster

For security and cleaning staff supplied by your own agency or by others:

1. **Duty roster and agencies > Agencies**: add the agency with licence numbers (a security agency needs its PSC licence), trade licence, contact and 24-hour number, contract dates, notice period, and the fee (flat per month, or per post per month). Upload licence, contract, insurance and police-verification documents.
2. **Posts and shifts**: define posts (Main gate, Roof, Lobby) with people needed per shift, and shifts with start and end times.
3. **Agency staff**: add each person with their agency employee number and NID.
4. **Roster**: assign people to posts and shifts for the week. **Copy last week** or **Suggest from last week** saves time. FlatWise warns about two shifts in one day, more than 12 hours, or seven days without a day off. **Print for the notice board** puts the week on the wall.
5. **Today**: mark each person Present, Late, Absent or On leave. For an absence press **Ask the agency for a replacement**, then **Add substitute** when someone comes.
6. **Attendance**: by month, by agency or by person: absences, missing shifts, days short.
7. **Bills**: choose agency and month. FlatWise shows the contract amount, the deduction for missing person-shifts and the expected bill. **Record invoice** with the agency's invoice number and amount. It becomes a normal expense under that agency as vendor and follows approval (two approvers above BDT 50,000); the ledger posts when it is paid.

## Reports across buildings

Each building has its own Collections, Dues and Finance screens. Compare occupancy on Portfolio. **Export CSV** on any list for a spreadsheet you can combine.

## Your own data

Your agency's staff and buildings are yours. Residents can ask to erase their personal details; invoices and ledgers stay without their names. If you leave FlatWise, export each building first.
