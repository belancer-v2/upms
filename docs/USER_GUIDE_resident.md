# User guide: flat owner, tenant and owner's representative

## Getting in

Your building admin gives you an email and a temporary password. Sign in, choose a new password when asked, and pick your language (English or বাংলা) and day or night colours at the top. Tap **?** next to a page title for help on that screen.

If you own more than one flat, or live in one and own another, you see all your flats. If you also sit on the committee, you see the committee screens too.

## My dues and payments

1. Open **My dues**. Each month shows the total, what is paid and the balance.
2. Pay by bKash or Nagad to the building's number, or by bank. Keep the message with the **transaction ID**.
3. Press **I paid** on the invoice: choose the method, enter the amount and the transaction ID. The treasurer checks it. When confirmed you get a **receipt** and the invoice turns Paid.
4. If your building has **online payment**, press **Pay now** instead: choose bKash, Nagad or card, and the receipt arrives by itself.
5. **Paid cash to the caretaker?** You will see a card *Cash you handed over*. Press **Yes, I paid** only if it is true. Or type the 6-digit code you got by SMS to the caretaker.
6. **Export CSV** on My dues downloads your own invoices to Excel.

## My flat

Your flat, owners and tenant. **Owners** can **Add my tenant** (then follow the tenant onboarding steps), approve letting a vacant flat, and move a tenant out. Tenants cannot change owners or letting.

## Tenant form (new tenants)

You will get an SMS with a link. No sign-in is needed. Fill five short steps on your phone, upload NID front and back and a photo, and send. The building office checks them. Only the building office and the police (as the law requires) see your details.

## Requests

**Service requests** > **Raise a request**: category (lift, water, electricity, security, cleaning, noise, other), a short title, what is wrong and where. Follow the updates, write comments, **Confirm and close** when fixed or **Reopen** (once) if not, and rate the fix 1 to 5.

## Notices and policies

**Notices** show what the committee published; opening one counts as read (opening the SMS link also counts). **Policies** are house rules: read and press **I have read and accept**. If a policy is updated you accept the new version.

## Visitors

- At the gate, the guard may ask you to **Approve** or **Deny** a visitor. You see it live under **Visitors > My visitors**.
- **Expect a visitor**: give a guest a 6-digit code for a time window; the guard types it and your guest comes in without waiting. The code is shown once and works once.
- **Regular visitors**: add your maid, driver, tutor or cook with dates; the guard can let them in without calling you.
- **Notifications** lists everyone who came to your flat.

## Hall and amenities, parking, rental records

- **Hall**: pick a free day on the calendar, request a slot, see the quote (fee, cleaning, generator, refundable deposit), and pay once the committee approves.
- **Parking**: your flat's slots, and **Register a vehicle** (plate, kind, sticker).
- **Rental records** (owners and tenants, if on): a private space for the agreement, monthly rent, repair issues and follow-ups. The admin and committee cannot see inside.
- **Profile**: photo and optional details (blood group, emergency contact). Nobody is forced to fill them.

## Your privacy

- You see only your own flat. Phone numbers and emails are shown only to people whose job needs them.
- There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
- To erase your personal details, open **Profile > Delete my account**. Your name, phone, email and photo are removed and your sign-in closed. Bills and payments stay in the building's books without your name.
