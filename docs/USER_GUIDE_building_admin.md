# User guide: building admin (Property Admin)

You run the building day to day: flats and people, the monthly bill, collections, staff and notices. This guide is in the order you will need it. Every screen also has a **?** button next to its title that opens a short help article, in English or Bangla.

## 1. The first afternoon

1. **Sign up** on the website (30-day free trial, no card). You become the property admin.
2. FlatWise opens the **setup wizard**. It has six short steps: building details, floors and flats, charges, due day and reminders, committee, review. Skip any step and come back. Your progress is saved, so you can continue on another phone.
3. Press **Finish** at the end. Until then the dashboard shows a checklist card.

## 2. Flats and people

- **Flats** > **Add flats**: *Generate* creates every flat from floors, blocks and flats per floor (A-101, A-102 ...). *Add one* adds a single flat. *Import* takes rows pasted from Excel (number, floor, block, area, bedrooms, owner name, owner phone). Running it again never makes duplicates.
- Tap a flat and **Add person**: owner or tenant, name (and Bangla name), phone, email. Tick *Create a sign-in for this person* to let them use FlatWise; a temporary password is shown **once**. Send it to them; they must choose a new one at first sign in.
- When someone leaves press **Move out**. The record stays in History, their sign-in for this building stops, and their vehicles are marked "to review".
- **To let**: a vacant flat can be offered to let, but only after its owner approves.
- **Building settings**: name (English and Bangla), address, floors. They print on every bill, notice and receipt.

## 3. The monthly bill

1. **Collections > Charges**: set what a flat pays (fixed per flat or per square foot). The wizard suggests typical Dhaka charges. Changing a charge only affects invoices you create later.
2. **Collections > Generate invoices**: month as `YYYY-MM`, due day 1 to 28. One invoice per flat; pressing it again creates nothing new. (You can also turn on automatic billing on the same page where available.)
3. Residents pay by bKash, Nagad, bank, cash, or online if you switched on **Online payment**. A payment a resident submits waits for the treasurer or you under **Payments**: compare the transaction id with the message, then **Confirm** or **Reject**. A payment you record yourself counts at once.
4. **Dues** shows who owes what, by how late. **Send reminders** (Standard and Pro) texts each person in their own language at the steps you chose. Each SMS costs one credit.
5. Every confirmed payment gets a receipt number and is posted to the ledger automatically.

## 4. Cash, expenses, staff

- **Cash desk**: the caretaker takes cash from a resident; the resident confirms by a 6-digit SMS code or in the app; the caretaker hands it to the treasurer, who accepts it. Nothing is lost between hands.
- **Finance > Expenses**: record a spend with category and vendor; it needs approval (two different approvers above BDT 50,000); then mark it paid. Paid expenses are posted to the ledger. **Budget** and **Assets** are on the same screen.
- **Staff and payroll**: add caretaker, guards and cleaners with National ID (stored encrypted; every time someone reveals a full number it is logged). Make a salary sheet each month, add the Eid bonus, have a different person approve, then pay and send payslips by SMS.
- **Duty roster and agencies**: for security or cleaning supplied by an agency (see the agency guide).

## 5. Telling people things

- **Notices**: write in English and Bangla, choose who it is for, pin it, and optionally SMS a link. You see how many have read it and can SMS the rest.
- **Policies**: house rules with versions. A new version needs everyone to accept again.
- **Service requests**: residents report problems; you assign them; each category has a deadline. **Committee** keeps members, meetings and votes.
- **Visitors, Parking, Hall and amenities**: gate log and passes, parking slots and vehicles, hall booking with approvals and fees (each needs its plan feature).

## 6. Tenants

**Tenant onboarding**: add the tenant (flat must be *To let*); they fill a form from an SMS link on their phone and upload NID and photo; you check each document and press *Everything is correct, add tenant*. Print the **DMP pack** for the police yourself; FlatWise never files anything.

## 7. SMS and email credits

Reminders, notices, OTP codes and payslips use credits. See the balance on **SMS and email credits**, set the low-balance alert and buy a pack online or by transfer.

## 8. Your data

- Any list has an **Export CSV** button (invoices, payments, dues, expenses, ledger, people, visitors).
- **Data export** (menu) downloads the whole building as a ZIP of CSV files, up to 3 times per day. Staff National ID numbers are never in it.
- You can see who did what in **Activity log**.
- To delete your own account you must first hand over the property admin role to someone else.

## 9. Month-end checklist

1. Generate next month's invoices.
2. Confirm pending payments; send reminders for dues.
3. Approve and pay expenses; lock the month's budget.
4. Prepare, approve and pay the salary sheet.
5. Check the cash desk is empty (all cash handed to the treasurer).
6. Open Finance > Ledger: it must say *Books balance*.
7. Print or export the collection and dues reports for the committee meeting.
