# User guide: caretaker and guard

Big buttons, Bangla first if you choose it, and it works on a low-cost phone. Pick বাংলা at the top.

## Caretaker

### Cash desk (money from residents)

1. Open **Cash desk**. At the top you see the **cash in hand** and the limit.
2. Press **Take cash**. Choose the flat and the unpaid month. Enter the amount you received.
3. Press **Take cash and send code**. The resident gets a 6-digit code by SMS. Ask them for it and type it. The entry becomes *Confirmed, in hand*.
4. If the resident has no phone, press **Take cash and ask payer to confirm**. They confirm in My dues.
5. When you are ready, press **Hand all to the treasurer**. The treasurer counts and presses *Accept cash*. Do not keep cash for days: the limit is on the page.
6. Wrong code three times locks that entry for an hour. The resident can still confirm in the app.

Never take cash without making an entry first. The resident's confirmation is your proof.

### Requests and expenses

- **Service requests**: you are told what is assigned to you. Press **Start work**, then **Mark resolved**, and write a comment. The resident confirms.
- **Finance**: you can ask for an expense (for example generator fuel). You see only your own requests.
- **Duty roster**: **Today** shows who is on duty. Tap a person to mark **Present**, **Late**, **Absent** or **On leave**. If someone is absent press **Ask the agency for a replacement**.
- **Staff** and **Tenant onboarding** are read-only for you where shown.

## Guard

### Visitor gate

1. Open **Visitors**. The **Gate** tab updates itself every 10 seconds.
2. **Walk-in guest**: press **New visitor**, choose the flat, type the name, phone, purpose, how many people and any vehicle, then **Ask the resident**. The resident approves or denies on their phone. When approved, press **Mark IN**; when they leave, **Mark OUT**.
3. **Delivery**: press **Delivery** and tap the flat. Residents are told; nobody has to approve.
4. **Guest with a code**: press **Pass code** and type the 6 digits. If it is valid for this time, mark them in.
5. **Regular helper** (maid, driver, tutor, cook): press **Regular visitors** and **Let in** while their dates are valid.
6. **Resident not answering**: press **Override**, write why, and let the visitor in. Your name and the reason are saved in the audit log. Use it only when needed.
7. Anyone inside for a long time is marked **Long stay**: check on them.

### Vehicles at the gate

**Parking > Gate**: type any part of a plate and press **Check**. If a registered vehicle matches you see the flat and slot. If not, treat it as a visitor and log it on a free visitor bay (**Log in** and **Log out**).

### Duty

Residents see who is on duty now. Check **Duty roster > Today** at the start of your shift and tell the caretaker if the roster is wrong.

## If something looks wrong

- You cannot see a button: your role or the building's plan does not include it. Ask the caretaker or admin.
- A resident says they paid cash: check the Cash desk entry; if there is none, tell the admin.
- Report a broken lift, water or light as a **Service request** with the flat or "no flat".
