# User guide: committee, treasurer and auditor

For President, Secretary, Treasurer, Members and the Auditor. You see the building's money and records. Each role has a little more or less power; the table says who can do what.

| Task | President | Secretary | Treasurer | Member | Auditor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| See collections, dues, expenses, ledger | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes (read only) |
| Confirm or record payments | no | no | yes | no | no |
| Accept cash handed over by the caretaker | no | no | yes | no | no |
| Request an expense | no | yes | yes | no | no |
| Approve an expense | yes | no | yes | no | no |
| Mark an expense paid | no | no | yes | no | no |
| Prepare a salary sheet | no | no | yes | no | no |
| Approve a salary sheet | yes | no | no | no | no |
| Show a full staff National ID (logged) | yes | no | no | no | no |
| Publish notices and policies | yes | yes | no | no | no |
| Add committee members, schedule meetings, close votes | yes | yes | no | no | no |
| Propose and vote | yes | yes | yes | yes | no |
| Download the whole building (ZIP) | yes | no | no | no | no |

(Building admins and agency managers can do all of the above except as noted.)

## Treasurer: the month

1. **Collections**: after the admin generates invoices, watch **Overview** (billed, collected, outstanding, rate, last six months, colour map of flats).
2. **Payments tab**: payments residents submitted wait for you. Compare the amount and transaction id with the bKash, Nagad or bank message, then **Confirm** (a receipt number is created and the ledger is posted) or **Reject**.
3. **Cash desk**: when the caretaker hands over cash, count it and press **Accept cash**, or **Not received** if it does not match.
4. **Finance > Expenses**: ask for expenses, mark approved ones **paid** and say how you paid. **Budget** compares plan and actual.
5. **Staff and payroll**: prepare the salary sheet; a different person must approve it.
6. **SMS and email credits**: keep a balance so reminders and OTP codes are sent.

## President and members

- **Approve expenses** (**Finance > Expenses**): the president and the treasurer approve; above BDT 50,000 two different people must approve. You see who asked and the reason.
- **Committee**: members and their tenure (a badge warns 90 days before a term ends), **meetings** (agenda, who attended, minutes, decisions) and **votes**: write a proposal, each member presses *Approve* or *Reject* once, then the president, secretary or admin presses **Close voting and lock the result**. The result is Approved, Rejected or Tied (not approved).
- **Service requests**: see what is open and what is overdue against the service level. Residents rate each fix.
- **Notices** and **Policies**: publish with read proof: you see how many have read it and who has not.
- **Tenant onboarding**: see cases; the admin or owner runs them.

## Secretary

Publishes notices and policies, schedules meetings and keeps minutes, adds committee members and staff, and can request expenses (the president or treasurer approves them).

## Auditor

Read-only view of billing, finance, ledger and reports. You cannot confirm, approve or change anything. Use **Finance > Ledger** (journal and trial balance) and **Activity log** to check the books. The ledger is append-only: entries are never edited, only reversed.

## Reports for the meeting

- **Collections > Overview** and **Dues**: who has paid, who has not, by how late.
- **Finance > Overview**: income, spending, surplus, cash and bank, budget against actual by category.
- **Finance > Ledger > Trial balance**: *Books balance* should show.
- **Export CSV** on any list for Excel. The president can also download the whole building from **Data export**.

## Good practice

- Do not approve something you asked for yourself.
- Confirm payments the same day. Residents wait for receipts.
- Read the Activity log monthly: it shows every confirmation, approval, override and export.
